# Shopify store policies — paste into admin

The MCP connector can't write legal policies (missing `write_legal_policies` scope),
so these need a one-time manual paste. They mirror what the site promises on
`/policies` (paid CA shipping from $12, free local pickup, 1–3 day dispatch,
30-day returns, hygiene exception) so the **checkout footer tells the same story
as the site**.

**Where:** Shopify admin → **Settings → Policies** (https://admin.shopify.com/store/shea-allnaturals/settings/legal)
Paste each block into its section and **Save**. For Privacy policy, the existing
Shopify template is fine — click **Replace with template** if it shows raw
`{{ placeholders }}`, which fills in the store name/contact automatically.

## Refund policy

We want you to love what you ordered. If something isn't right, contact us within **30 days of delivery** at allnaturals@allnaturalscosmetics.ca or 705-719-2750.

**Eligibility**
- **Unopened products** in their original condition are eligible for a full refund or exchange.
- For hygiene reasons, **opened products** can't be returned — unless they arrived damaged or faulty, in which case we'll make it right with a replacement or refund.
- Bulk & wholesale orders: please contact us to arrange returns; restocking terms may apply.

**How it works**
1. Email us with your order number and the reason for the return.
2. We'll confirm eligibility and send you the return address and instructions.
3. Once we receive and inspect the items, your refund is issued to the original payment method within 5–10 business days.

Return shipping costs are the customer's responsibility unless the product arrived damaged or incorrect.
